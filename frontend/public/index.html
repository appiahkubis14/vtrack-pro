Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to buy new vehicles?

No. You can add vehicles you already own. Fit a tracker, add the vehicle and its rider, and it appears on your dashboard.

Which trackers work with it?

Common GT06, TK103 and Teltonika devices. We can advise on which one suits your vehicles and SIM network.

Can riders pay by mobile money?

Yes. Payments by cash, mobile money and bank are recorded in cedis against the rider and the day, and roll into daily settlements.

Will it lock a vehicle while someone is riding?

No. The system warns first and only locks when the vehicle is stopped. Every lock and unlock is logged with a reason and who approved it.

Who can see what?

Admins see everything. Hub managers and dispatchers see their own hubs. Finance sees money. Investors see returns. Riders see their own day. A vehicle used privately can hide its position outside working hours.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Lists and reports can be exported, and your history stays available for owners, banks and insurers.

How much does it cost?

It depends on fleet size and hardware. Send a request and we will reply with a clear quote.
