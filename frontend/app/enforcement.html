Lock Control

Grace periods, safe locks and unlocks. A vehicle is never locked while it is moving.

How it works. When a day closes with a shortfall, the rider gets a warning SMS and a grace period starts (the contract's setting, otherwise the hub's, otherwise 12 hours). If it ends unpaid, the vehicle is locked only when it is stationary. Paying the shortfall releases it automatically. Manual locks need an administrator's approval; unlocking needs a reason.
